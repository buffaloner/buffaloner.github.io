## Pod-O-Mator

Having a background in software engineering support and believing in the true spirit of the DevOps philosophy, making life less miserable. How does this embody the true spirit of the DevOps philosophy you may ask? Think about it - DevOps at its core is about empathy. You break down silos because whether or not they were built with purpose, they are meant to segregate people from each other and not allow for finding the humanity in the pursuit of optimizing productivity. At the heart of any engineer is to figure out how to help your team get things done quickly.

I don't believe being productive and being empathetic are mutually exclusive. I believe the biggest advantages come in figuring out the best systems of organization. In groups, its the complement of thinking about efficiency and seeing how the lack of it causes suffering.

For this project, I had to figure out the best workflow for creating designs and keeping track of what to put them on.

A champion of open source, and a deep need to find a creative outlet, I created a POD store. Having more time on my hands than I would have cared to have, I leaned into the new AI vibe coding paradigm and worked on a rough outline of my idea, getting quality feedback after giving garbage prompts, and spending a while figuring out how everything goes together in this _Brave New World_...
